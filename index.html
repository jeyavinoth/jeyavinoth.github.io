04 / About

I'm a software and data engineer, about ten years in. Most of my work is taking data that's messy, huge, or just plain weird and building the systems that get it into production and keep it there. I approach it as an engineer, real code and real systems, and I spend most of my energy on the parts that decide whether a pipeline survives contact with real data: retries, the weird edge cases, the stuff that pages you at 2am. I came up through the modeling and analytics side too, so the statistical and ML work is as much mine as the systems engineering.
